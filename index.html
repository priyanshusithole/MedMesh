Geriatric Safety & Timing Principles

1. Nocturia Fall Prevention

Diuretics (water pills like Furosemide/Lasix) are strictly restricted to morning 8:00 AM. This prevents nighttime urination, disorientation in the dark, and catastrophic elderly hip fractures.

2. Chelation Binding Spacing

Calcium and iron supplements are spaced 4+ hours away from thyroid and antibiotics. When taken together, minerals chemically bind with drugs and eliminate their absorption.

3. Circadian Statin Timing

Cholesterol synthesis by the liver peaks between midnight and 04:00 AM. Statins (Atorvastatin) are timed for 10:00 PM Bedtime to coincide with maximum enzyme activity.
