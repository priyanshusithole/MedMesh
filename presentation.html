01 / The Challenge

Problem Statement

The Silent Epidemic of Polypharmacy

Fragmented Multi-Doctor Regimens

Elderly patients frequently take 5 to 15 concurrent medications prescribed by independent specialists (Cardiology, Endocrinology, Orthopedics) who lack unified cross-communication.

Hidden Duplicate Active Salts

Different pharmaceutical brands mask identical active molecules (e.g., Augmentin vs Clavam, Janumet vs Galvus Met), leading to unintentional toxicity and fatal liver/renal overload.

Illegible Handwritten Prescriptions

Doctors' handwritten cursive is notoriously difficult for patients and caregivers to decipher, leading to missed doses, incorrect dosing intervals, and drug administration errors.

Critical Interactions & Fall Hazards

Severe drug interactions (e.g., Warfarin + NSAID internal bleeding) and mis-timed diuretics (taken in late evening causing nocturia and midnight fall injuries) drive high hospital readmissions.

Clinical Urgency: Over 50% of preventable geriatric hospitalizations stem from unmonitored polypharmacy interactions and duplicate salt overdoses.

03 / Engineering

Technology Stack

Robust, Zero-Bundle & Scalable Architecture

Frontend Layer

  • Vanilla HTML5 & Modern ES6+: Zero-bundle overhead, ultra-fast initial load on low-spec hardware.
  • Tailwind CSS (JIT): Earth tone palette (Bitter Olive, Olive Leaf, Emerald) with native print styling.
  • Lucide Icons: Strict zero-emoji medical-grade iconography.
  • RFC 5545 iCalendar Engine: Pure client-side Blob generation for recurring `.ics` calendar sync.

Backend & Runtime

  • FastAPI (Python 3.10+): High-throughput asynchronous ASGI microservice framework.
  • Pydantic v2: Strict clinical data modeling and validation schemas.
  • Vercel Serverless Functions: Zero-maintenance edge routing via api/index.py and vercel.json.
  • HTTPX & Pillow: Async API requests and prescription image normalization.

AI & Knowledge Bases

  • Google Gemini 2.5 Flash: Multimodal handwriting OCR and compassionate vernacular briefings.
  • NIH RxNorm & NLM APIs: International drug-drug interaction surveillance and RxCUI mapping.
  • Curated Brand Engine: 100+ mapped regional pharmaceutical trade names.
  • Streaming TTS Pipeline: Chunked MP3 streaming eliminating missing Hindi voice packs.

04 / Core Differentiators

Key Innovations

Beyond Standard Pill Trackers

Clinical OCR Transcription Framing

Solves the widespread LLM safety-refusal issue on prescriptions (e.g. Adderall XR, psychotropics) by framing prompts as an objective Document Transcription Engine, achieving zero false refusals and high handwriting accuracy.

Zero-Refusal Multimodal OCR

Affectionate Cultural Caregiver Voice

Medical jargon causes senior patient anxiety. MedMesh automatically de-jargonizes clinical risks into empathetic Hindi and English briefings using culturally respectful honorifics (Dadi ji, Beta, Uncle).

Cultural Vernacular Empathy

Zero-Install Fall-Safe Calendar Sync

Instead of forcing users to install proprietary mobile apps, MedMesh exports native RFC 5545 .ics files that configure recurring daily alarms and pre-dose notifications directly on Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, and Outlook.

Native RFC 5545 .ICS Pipeline
